# Credits

- [Universal Animation Library](https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-animation-library) and [Universal Animation Library 2](https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-animation-library-2) (every human animation)
- Poly Pizza: [Wolf](https://poly.pizza/m/P1gU3Qkr9r) (neutral creeps, with its own animations), [Dragon](https://poly.pizza/m/VBvzjFIYws) (the neutral boss, with its own animations), [Armor Metal](https://poly.pizza/m/TMUoxILh9w), [Shield](https://poly.pizza/m/srN1KGAO7f), [Shield Round](https://poly.pizza/m/lWajrVXcnA), [Spear](https://poly.pizza/m/fH1zmvjPNx), [Sword](https://poly.pizza/m/ajOJ2NLz5m), [Axe](https://poly.pizza/m/W0UYZPYSXf), [Axe](https://poly.pizza/m/xEhKbBI0Yp), [Axe Small](https://poly.pizza/m/o54NXjRI4V) and [Doublesided Hammer](https://poly.pizza/m/UIXvQ73DS1) (armour, shields and weapons)

The ground and tree textures are built by `tools/assets` from photo scans released under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/):

- [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com) ([licence](https://polyhaven.com/license)): `forrest_ground_01`, `brown_mud_dry`, `sand_01`, `forest_leaves_04`, `ganges_river_pebbles` (ground), `castle_wall_slates`, `medieval_blocks_05`, `plastered_wall`, `rough_wood`, `weathered_brown_planks`, `thatch_roof_angled`, `roof_09`, `roof_slates_02`, `medieval_wood`, `farm_furrows` (buildings), `large_sandstone_blocks_01`, `grey_roof_tiles_02` (dressed stone and slate roofs), `rock_boulder_dry` (mines and quarries), `kloofendal_48d_partly_cloudy_puresky` (sky light), `pine_bark`, `bark_brown_02` (bark) and the twig atlas of `fir_tree_01` (pine needles)
